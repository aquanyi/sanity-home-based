Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

How are your tutors selected and vetted?
Every S.H.T.A educator goes through a strict 4-step interview process. This includes verifying their academic credentials, subject-matter proficiency exams, a pedagogical teaching demonstration, and thorough background checks to ensure child safety.
Do you offer both online and in-person tutoring?
Yes, we provide flexible options. For families located in Nairobi, we offer convenient in-home sessions. For international students or those who prefer digital learning, we offer highly interactive online sessions utilizing modern whiteboard software.
How quickly will I see improvement in my child's grades?
While every child learns at their own pace, the vast majority of our parents report noticeable improvements in both confidence and academic scores within the first 4 to 6 weeks of consistent sessions.
What if my child doesn't connect with their assigned tutor?
Student-tutor synergy is crucial for success. If for any reason the match isn't a perfect fit, let us know after the first session and we will gladly reassign a new educator at no additional cost.
